It learns your grinder

Tell Crema which way finer turns on each grinder, and the coach names the next number based on how that grinder has actually responded. It won't overshoot from sour to bitter, and it drops advice that hasn't worked twice.

Beyond grind, it can suggest ratio, pre-infusion, dose for your basket, puck prep or temperature. Every claim says how many shots it's based on.
